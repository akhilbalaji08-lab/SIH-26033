## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
1. Detailed Explanation of the Proposed Solution
KRISHISETU is an intelligent agricultural trading and distribution ecosystem that connects farmers, FPOs, consumers, retailers, and transportation providers through a single digital platform.

Unlike traditional agricultural marketplaces, KRISHISETU does not simply connect buyers and sellers. It intelligently coordinates the entire journey of agricultural products, from harvesting to final delivery.

Its unique features include:

Smart Farmer Clusters: Groups nearby farmers growing similar crops to combine their produce and negotiate better prices.

Reverse Bidding Marketplace: Buyers publish their requirements, and farmers or FPOs submit competitive offers.

AI Price Intelligence: Predicts potential selling prices using historical prices, demand, seasonal patterns, and market data.

Virtual Agricultural Warehouse: Displays available produce across nearby farms and storage facilities without requiring a physical warehouse.

Smart Load Sharing: Combines orders from different buyers to maximize vehicle capacity and reduce transportation costs.

Digital Product Passport: Maintains product information, including farm origin, harvest date, quality details, and delivery history.

Food Waste Rescue System: Automatically identifies surplus or unsold produce and recommends nearby buyers or food processing units.

2. How It Addresses the Problem
Reduces unnecessary intermediaries through direct digital trading.

Combines small farmers' produce to enable bulk sales.

Helps farmers negotiate competitive prices.

Reduces transportation expenses through shared deliveries.

Connects surplus produce with buyers before it gets wasted.

Improves transparency through digital product records.

3. Innovation and Uniqueness of the Solution
The following features make KRISHISETU different from ordinary agricultural marketplace applications:

   Feature                                 Innovation

Reverse bidding                   Buyers compete to source agricultural produce

## Feasibility and Viability
1. Analysis of the Feasibility of the Idea
KRISHISETU can be developed using existing web technologies, machine learning algorithms, mapping services, and cloud infrastructure.

A working prototype can initially focus on:

>Farmer and buyer registration.

>Product listing and reverse bidding.

>Farmer clustering.

>Basic AI price prediction.

>Shared delivery planning.

>Advanced blockchain and IoT integration can be introduced in later stages.

2. Potential Challenges and Risks
>Farmers may have limited digital knowledge.

>Agricultural prices fluctuate frequently.

>Coordinating multiple farmers and buyers can be difficult.

>Produce quality may vary.

>Logistics availability may be limited.

3. Strategies for Overcoming These Challenges
>Introduce regional-language support and voice-based assistance.

>Use regularly updated market data.

>Verify farmers and buyers.

>Implement quality grading and digital records.

>Partner with local transport providers and FPOs.
